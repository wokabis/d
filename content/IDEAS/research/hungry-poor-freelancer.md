[ ] Day 1: Choose one niche
Define the target as small web design and marketing agencies. Write a one-sentence offer and list your assumptions.

[ ] Day 2: Gather market evidence
Review public complaints, competing products, and current workarounds. Record 20 relevant pieces of evidence with sources.

[ ] Day 3: Interview prospects
Approach 10 agency owners. Aim for real conversations, not survey responses or likes.

[ ] Day 4: Narrow the problem
Choose the most painful recurring problem supported by interviews. Write a fixed-scope pilot offer.

[ ] Day 5: Build a working demo
Create a lead tracker, overdue-follow-up view, message templates, and a sample weekly report using synthetic data.

[ ] Day 6: Create a simple sales page
Explain the problem, deliverables, timeline, pilot price, and how to contact you. Use free hosting or share the demo directly.

[ ] Day 7: Make the first offers
Contact qualified prospects individually and ask for short calls. Try to secure your first paid pilot.

[ ] Day 8: Follow up and refine
Respond to questions, follow up appropriately, and update the offer based on objections.

[ ] Day 9: Close a pilot
Agree on scope, access, payment, and delivery expectations. Do not start handling sensitive customer data without safeguards.

[ ] Day 10: Deliver the core workflow
Configure the CRM or spreadsheet and test it against realistic sample cases before using live data.

[ ] Day 11: Measure usefulness
Check whether overdue tasks are identified correctly, the workflow saves time, and the customer understands the report.

[ ] Day 12: Request feedback and a referral
Ask what was useful, what failed, and whether the customer would pay for continued support.

[ ] Day 13: Document and automate
Turn repeated delivery steps into scripts and reusable templates. Automate only stable, proven steps.

[ ] Day 14: Make the next decision
Review sales, collected revenue, delivery time, customer feedback, and remaining expenses. Continue, change the niche, or revise the offer based on evidence.
