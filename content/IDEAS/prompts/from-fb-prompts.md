Identify the 3 highest-impact strategic opportunities for the next 12 months, explain the reasoning behind each, and provide a clear action plan with priorities, risks, and key metrics.”


2️⃣ Market Research Report

“Act as a senior market research analyst. I’m entering [market] with [offer]. Analyze the market size, customer segments, demand trends, unmet needs, competitors, barriers to entry, and key market shifts. Identify the most attractive opportunity, the biggest risks, and the assumptions I should validate before investing.”


3️⃣ Competitor Teardown

“Act as a competitive intelligence strategist. Analyze my top competitors: [list]. Compare their positioning, pricing, offers, strengths, weaknesses, customer acquisition channels, messaging, and key differentiators. Identify underserved customer needs and realistic market gaps I could use to build a stronger competitive position.”

4️⃣ Pricing Strategy Audit

“Act as a pricing strategist. My offer is [describe] and currently costs [price]. Evaluate my pricing based on customer value, competitors, target market, positioning, and willingness to pay. Identify my pricing power and recommend a pricing structure, packaging, or offer changes that could increase revenue without unnecessarily hurting conversion.”

5️⃣ Customer Segmentation

“Act as a customer segmentation strategist. Analyze my business [describe] and customer base [describe]. Identify the 3 most valuable customer segments based on need, buying intent, revenue potential, and accessibility. For each segment, define their pain points, buying triggers, objections, willingness to pay, and best acquisition channels. Then recommend where to focus first and why.”


6️⃣ Strategic SWOT Analysis

“Act as a senior strategic business analyst. Create a specific, evidence-based SWOT analysis for [business]. Avoid generic observations. For every weakness, provide a practical corrective action; for every threat, provide a mitigation strategy; and for every opportunity, provide the first concrete step to capture it. End with the 3 priorities that should receive the most attention.”

7️⃣ Go-To-Market Strategy

“Act as a senior go-to-market strategist. I’m launching [product] for [target audience] in [market]. Build a practical GTM strategy covering positioning, ideal customer profile, offer, messaging, acquisition channels, launch sequence, conversion strategy, and a realistic plan to acquire the first 100 customers. Include key milestones, metrics, and the biggest risks to watch.”

8️⃣ Strategic Growth Plan

“Act as a senior growth strategist. My business currently generates [amount] from [offer], and my goal is to reach [target] within [timeframe]. Build a practical growth plan covering revenue levers, customer acquisition, retention, new markets, offers, pricing, and distribution. Identify the biggest constraint limiting growth, explain why it matters, and give me the first 5 actions I should take, ranked by impact and effort.”
